3 · Settings & calibration

Calibrate: load an image of a sample with known PCT, type its value, press Add. With 2+ points the PCT curve (log-linear) and the 0.5 / 2 ng/mL boundaries are fitted automatically. Without calibration the result is only a rough estimate.
Classification (PCT, ng/mL)
● LOW < 0.5 – sepsis unlikely, low risk
● MID 0.5 – 2 – sepsis possible, moderate risk; re-test in 6–24 h
● HIGH ≥ 2 – sepsis likely, high risk (≥ 10: severe sepsis / septic shock likely)

Research prototype – not a diagnostic device. Always confirm with a validated lab test and clinical assessment.
